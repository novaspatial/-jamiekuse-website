About

aaadrian is Adrian, a young Canadian songwriter who wrote and recorded his debut album, Snack Pack, when he was eight. The songs grew out of silly phrases and melodies he made up and sang with his friends at school, turning snack-time favourites into big hooks, jokes and sing-along choruses. He writes his own lyrics, and no AI was used anywhere in making the music.

His little brother Westley sings backing vocals on every track, and Adrian filled the record with friends and cousins, plus a hidden cameo from Canadian rapper Snak the Ripper, who joins his son on "food fight."
