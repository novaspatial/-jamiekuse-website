About

Jamie Kuse is an audio engineer and producer with over two decades of industry experience. His work spans commercial releases, global broadcast, and live events, from JUNO Award–winning albums to the world stage of FIFA and Valve's The International, with productions amassing over 1 billion streams worldwide. Credits include records with Snotty Nose Rez Kids, NAV, KILLY, Tyla Yaweh, Dax, Snak The Ripper and Boslen (full credits).

He is the co-founder of the Emmy Award–winning NOVA Studios, one of Canada's premier Dolby Atmos facilities; NOVA Post, whose client roster spans Google, Nintendo, Valve, and Porsche; and The Immersive Preservation Project, which records Indigenous language, song and story in immersive sound with communities across Canada.

His focus today is Dolby Atmos: immersive mixes for artists and labels, immersive audio for cultural centres and exhibits, and hands-on workshops where communities hear, record and keep immersive audio of their own.
